Why not just use AI yourself?

You can, and for plenty of things you should. AI tools are genuinely good at producing code quickly. I use them every day, and they're a big part of why I can deliver in days rather than weeks.

But typing the code was never the hard part. The hard part is:

  • Knowing what to build, and what to leave out
  • Spotting code that looks right but isn't: the missed edge case, the security gap, the query that slows to a crawl once real data arrives
  • Fitting changes into an existing system without breaking what already works
  • Keeping the codebase understandable as it grows, instead of piling generated code on top of generated code
  • Owning the result when something goes wrong

AI gives you speed. I give you that speed with experience, judgement and accountability behind it.

Running on an older .NET system?

Many businesses depend on systems built years ago: .NET Framework, Web Forms, WCF services, VB.NET, and a SQL Server database that has grown along with the business. They still work, but every change feels risky, and fewer and fewer people know how they fit together.

I've spent over 20 years working on systems like these. Modern AI tools also make it much quicker to read, map and explain a large, older codebase than it used to be, and I check everything they tell me against the code itself.

My approach is steady and low-risk:

  • Map what you have, and document how it really works
  • Put tests around the behaviour your business relies on, so any change is caught
  • Modernise piece by piece, with the system running throughout. No big‑bang rewrite
  • Move to modern .NET and Azure at a pace that suits your business

Modernisation can run as part of a subscription, or as a fixed-price project.

Your questions answered.

How many hours do I get each month?

I don't bill by the hour, and I don't track hours. Hourly billing is an inefficient and unpredictable way to work, for both of us.

It's tempting to compare on hourly rates, but that's comparing apples with oranges. More hours don't mean more value, and with AI tools, hours spent are a poorer measure of output than ever.

My focus is on delivering consistently high-quality results, with as little risk to you as possible. Without that high bar, more hours of work just means more code to fix later.

I know hourly billing is the norm in this industry, and this may be a leap you're not yet comfortable with. That's why I offer the money-back guarantee. Try it and see for yourself.

Well then, how many issues will you finish each month?

It's hard to say, because issues vary enormously in size. “Improve validation on our checkout page” is very different from “Build an integration with this third-party API”.

I can't tell you how many issues I'll finish (and it isn't a very useful measure anyway), but I can promise a steady stream of updates and progress from me. No more developers who “go dark” for weeks or months at a time.

Each week, I'll ship something of value to you. Larger pieces of work are split up and delivered in stages, so you see progress early and get consistent value from the subscription.

Can you join our daily stand-up or weekly check-in call?

No. I keep meetings to a minimum; it's a big part of why I can deliver as quickly as I do. My process is fully transparent, so routine check-ins aren't needed.

You'll always be able to see exactly what I'm working on. And if something genuinely needs a conversation, I'll arrange a short call.

I really need to explain something to you though.

That's fine. Context is always welcome, and the more I know about what you'd like built, the better. Write it up in the task, and if anything is still unclear, I'll arrange a brief call.

Can I call you if my site goes down?

I don't offer emergency support; that's best handled by someone on your team. Ideally, for security reasons, I wouldn't have access to your production environment at all.

Do you use AI to write the code?

Yes, where it helps: drafting code, writing tests and getting to grips with unfamiliar code. It's a big part of how I deliver quickly.

Nothing reaches your codebase without my review. I only use AI tools whose terms keep your code private, and if your business has its own AI policy, I'll follow it.
